About this database. This database presents candidate measurement instruments for Aneurysm Obliteration, one of the aSAH core outcome domains. Mapping categories are proposed for discussion and are not consensus-endorsed. The instrument descriptions and references are sourced from the supplied Aneurysm Obliteration CSV. Domain Match and Feasibility assessment fields are available for you to populate.
